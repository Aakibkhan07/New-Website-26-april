❓ The Basics

What is Algorithmic Trading?

Algorithmic trading (also called algo trading or automated trading) means using a computer program — called an algorithm — to automatically buy and sell financial instruments based on a set of pre-defined rules.

Instead of you manually watching charts and clicking "Buy" or "Sell", the software does it for you — in milliseconds — based on rules you (or we) define in advance.

💡 Think of it like a vending machine for trading: you set the rules once, and the machine executes them perfectly every time — no hesitation, no emotion, no mistakes.

📖 Key Concepts

Terms Every Algo Trader Should Know

Δ Delta
How much an option's price moves when the underlying (Nifty) moves by ₹1. A delta of 0.5 means the option gains ₹0.50 for every ₹1 move in Nifty. Algo strategies use delta to stay market-neutral.
Θ Theta (Time Decay)
The daily loss in an option's value as it approaches expiry. Options sellers profit from theta — the option "decays" towards zero. Many TradeMetrix strategies are theta-based, earning from time decay.
Γ Gamma
How fast delta changes as the market moves. High gamma near expiry means small moves cause big swings. Gamma scalping strategies profit from large moves regardless of direction.
🏗️ Strategy / Algorithm
A complete set of rules — entry conditions, exit conditions, stop-loss, position size — programmed into software. Once set, the algo follows these rules precisely without deviation. This is what TradeMetrix sells.
🔬 Backtesting
Running a strategy on historical data (e.g. 5 years of Nifty data) to see how it would have performed — before risking real money. A must-do step before going live with any algo strategy.
📉 Drawdown
The maximum peak-to-trough decline in your capital before recovery. A strategy with 30% max drawdown means your capital could fall 30% before recovering. Lower drawdown = safer strategy.
📊 Open Interest (OI)
Total number of outstanding options contracts at a given strike price. Rising OI at a strike = more market participants at that level. Option chain scanners track OI to find support/resistance zones.
🛡️ Stop-Loss (SL)
A pre-set price at which a losing trade automatically closes to cap your loss. The most important risk tool in trading. Algo strategies enforce SL automatically — no human override possible.
📐 Straddle / Strangle
Options strategies that involve buying or selling both a Call and a Put at the same (straddle) or different (strangle) strike prices. Used to profit from large moves (buy) or low volatility (sell). Popular for weekly expiry trading.
💡 Myths vs Reality

Common Misconceptions About Algo Trading

Don't let myths stop you from exploring one of the most powerful trading tools available.

❌
Myth
"Algo trading is only for large institutions and hedge funds with crores of capital."
✅
Reality
Today's algo platforms like TradeMetrix are built for individual retail traders. You can start with a single Nifty lot (~₹50,000 margin) and run fully automated strategies.
❌
Myth
"You need to know Python, coding, or have a tech background to use algo software."
✅
Reality
Our platform has 50+ ready-to-use strategies. You configure risk parameters through a UI — no coding required. If you want to build your own strategies, we have a drag-and-drop Strategy Builder.
❌
Myth
"Algo trading guarantees profits — if the system trades automatically, it must make money."
✅
Reality
Algos execute rules — they don't guarantee profits. Markets are uncertain. A good algo trades consistently and manages risk well, but losses are part of trading. Backtesting shows historical performance, not future guaranteed returns.
❌
Myth
"Algo trading is the same as getting tips or advisory calls — someone is managing my money."
✅
Reality
Algo software is a tool, not a service. Nobody manages your account. You choose which strategy to run, set your risk limits, and your money stays in your own broker account at all times. TradeMetrix is pure software.
