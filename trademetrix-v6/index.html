📖 Algo Trader's Lexicon

Key Terms Every Algo Trader Needs

The Greeks
[Δ Delta]
Movement Correlation
Used to stay market-neutral. Measures how much an option price moves per ₹1 move in the underlying.
[Θ Theta]
Time Decay
The core engine of option selling. Options lose value every day — our ThetaPro strategy captures this decay automatically.
[Γ Gamma]
Rate of Delta Change
Used for scalping large moves. Highest near expiry — GammaPro exploits rapid Gamma expansion on expiry day.
Core Concepts
[Backtesting]
5+ years historical data check. Risk-free validation before risking any real capital.
[Drawdown]
Max peak-to-trough decline limit. TradeMetrix enforces this automatically — no human override possible.
[Open Interest (OI)]
Outstanding contracts scanner for support/resistance. Our Option Chain Scanner tracks OI buildup live.
[Stop-Loss (SL)]
Hard-coded exit. No human override. The algo never moves a SL "just this once" — it executes the rule you set.
[Straddle/Strangle]
Multi-leg automated execution logic. Buy/sell both CE and PE simultaneously with one click. Fully automated by our engine.
[PCR Ratio]
Put-Call Ratio — institutional sentiment indicator. Our scanner tracks PCR in real-time to identify reversal setups.

Do you provide tips, signals, or buy/sell calls?
▾
Absolutely not. We are pure-play technology. No WhatsApp tips, no buy/sell calls, no SEBI-registered advisory services. You pre-program your own rules — we execute them. We are not affiliated with NSE, BSE, or SEBI.
Can I cancel anytime?
▾
Yes. Access continues until the end of the billing period with zero surprise charges. No lock-in contracts. For monthly plans, cancel before the next billing date. For longer plans, access runs until expiry.
Is my broker API key secure?
▾
All API credentials are AES-256 encrypted before storage — never plain text. API keys are granted trade+read permissions only — never withdrawal or transfer permissions. Your funds remain 100% in your own broker account. TradeMetrix cannot move, access, or withdraw your money under any circumstances.
Do I need coding knowledge?
▾
None at all. 50+ ready-to-use strategy templates are available. You configure lot size, stop-loss, target, and capital through a visual interface. The drag-and-drop Strategy Builder (Beta) lets you build custom logic without writing a single line of code.
What happens when I hit my daily loss limit?
▾
The kill-switch fires automatically — all new orders stop, and open positions are optionally squared off. This is enforced at the software level and cannot be overridden mid-session. The algo never "breaks the rules" the way a human does.
